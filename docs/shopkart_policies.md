# ShopKart Policies

## Returns

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for most products. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags and accessories intact.

Electronics can be returned only if the item arrives damaged or defective. Such issues must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with photographs of the damage.

Sale items, innerwear, and personal care products are not returnable under any circumstances.

## Refunds

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after the returned item passes quality check.

For cash on delivery orders, refunds are issued as a bank transfer. Customers must provide bank account details, and the transfer takes 7 to 10 working days.

Shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is due to a damaged or wrong item.

## Shipping

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days in metro cities and 5 to 8 working days elsewhere. Express delivery is available in selected pin codes for an extra charge and delivers within 2 working days.

Orders above 499 rupees qualify for free standard shipping. Below that, a flat shipping fee of 49 rupees applies.

## Order cancellation

Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before they are shipped. Once shipped, an order cannot be cancelled, but it can be returned after delivery under the returns policy.

## Warranty

Electronics carry a manufacturer warranty of 12 months from the date of delivery. Warranty claims are handled by the manufacturer's service centre, not by ShopKart.

Warranty does not cover physical damage, water damage, or unauthorised repairs.
